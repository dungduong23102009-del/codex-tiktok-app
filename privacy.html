Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

1. Overview

Codex Motion Video Publisher is a personal creative workflow for producing and preparing videos for TikTok. TikTok integration is currently under development.

2. Planned TikTok Integration

The planned integration will use TikTok Login Kit to request authorization from the account owner. The requested permissions may include user.info.basic and video.upload.

3. Purpose of Data Access

TikTok account information will be used to identify the authorized account. Video upload permissions will be used to submit creator-approved content to TikTok.

4. Authorization

The planned integration requires the account owner's authorization. The account owner can revoke access through TikTok's connected-app settings.

5. Data Storage and Security

Data storage and token-handling procedures are being designed as part of development. This policy will be updated with the actual retention, protection, and deletion practices before the integration is released.

6. Third-Party Services

The planned integration will communicate with TikTok's official APIs. TikTok's handling of information is governed by its own privacy policies.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the project's functionality and data practices are finalized.
